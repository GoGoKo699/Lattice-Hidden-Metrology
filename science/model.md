# Model and research contract

We investigate estimation of a passive optical phase rotation from a supplied ensemble of coherent-state probes with hidden correlations. The observer receives calibration samples from the same source and phase-encoded sensing samples. Both computationally bounded and unbounded observers receive identical optical and classical resources.

Let \(X\in\mathbb R^d\) be a classical displacement label drawn from a distribution \(P\), and prepare \(|\alpha(X)\rangle\) in bosonic modes, with the preparation map and energy budget specified explicitly. A passive interferometer \(U_\theta\) produces \(U_\theta|\alpha(X)\rangle\). Calibration copies have \(\theta=0\); sensing copies share an unknown \(\theta\). No observer is given the hidden lattice secret. Measurements, collective quantum processing and classical postprocessing must be charged.

**Open specification:** fix the exact homogeneous continuous-LWE distribution, noise and scaling, phase action, prior, calibration and sensing copy counts, precision of classical output, and energy scaling before asserting an asymptotic theorem. The source is supplied; this is not a lower bound against an observer allowed to replace it with arbitrary probe states.
