# Lattice Hidden Metrology

**Research question:** Can a passive optical sensor preserve substantial information about an unknown phase while recovering a useful estimate remains computationally hard under lattice assumptions?

This repository currently contains an **initial research model and proof-obligation map**, not a completed theorem or validated security claim. Its intended contribution is a conditional separation between optical information and computationally accessible estimation with equal calibration access.

## Scientific account

- [Model and access contract](science/model.md)
- [Candidate results and decisive proof gaps](science/claims-and-gaps.md)
- [Related-work audit](references/RELATED_WORK.md)

The research must distinguish the optical mixture's Fisher information from conditional coherent-state Fisher information, and distinguish hardness for classical samples from hardness against arbitrary collective quantum measurements. Neither step is assumed for free.

## Scope

The source of coherent probes is supplied and may carry hidden lattice correlations. Arbitrary substitute probe preparation is outside the proposed lower bound. Optical energy, calibration copies, phase prior, measurement precision and all computational access must be explicit before any theorem is promoted.
